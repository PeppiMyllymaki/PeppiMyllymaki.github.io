About Me

I’m a soon-to-graduate Bachelor of Engineering student in Game Programming at XAMK, with a multidisciplinary background spanning web development, digital communication, game development, graphic design, and 3D content creation.

My experience combines both technical and creative work. I have developed responsive websites for real clients using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Wix, and other web platforms, while also working with UI/UX, accessibility, content structure, and visual design. Alongside web development, I have hands-on experience in digital marketing and social media, creating campaigns, graphics, short-form videos, written content, and promotional materials for platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and LinkedIn.

My game development background includes C#, C++, Unity, Unreal Engine, 2D game development, gameplay systems, UI, narrative systems, and creative direction. I have also worked extensively with 3D modeling, photogrammetry, digital reconstruction, and technical art, including the creation and processing of more than 60 3D models for a digital reconstruction project.

I enjoy working at the intersection of technology, visual communication, and creativity. Whether I’m building a website, developing an interactive system, creating a visual identity, producing digital content, or working with 3D assets, I’m interested in finding practical and visually engaging ways to bring ideas to life.

I’m comfortable working independently as well as collaborating with clients and multidisciplinary teams, and I bring a combination of technical problem-solving, visual thinking, communication skills, and a willingness to learn to my projects.

I worked on the Game Tech Kymenlaakso project at Xamk, which focuses on using game technologies, 3D modelling and virtual environments to develop Kotka’s digital and virtual city model. The project explores how these technologies can support areas such as urban planning, digital twins and new virtual experiences.

My role focused on photogrammetry and the creation of 3D models of statues and artworks located around Kotka. I used Epic Games’ RealityScan to capture real-world objects and generate detailed 3D scans from photographic data. This required planning the scanning process, capturing suitable imagery from different angles and ensuring that the objects were documented accurately enough to produce usable digital models.

After generating the scans, I processed and refined the models in Blender. This included cleaning up unwanted geometry, fixing imperfections and making adjustments to improve the overall quality and usability of the models. I worked with the models through multiple stages of the workflow, from real-world capture to a finished digital asset suitable for use in virtual environments.

The work gave me practical experience with photogrammetry, 3D asset creation and the technical challenges involved in converting real-world cultural objects into digital form. It also allowed me to contribute to a larger project focused on building a virtual representation of Kotka and exploring new applications for 3D and game technologies.

What I did

  • Created 60+ 3D models of statues and artworks located around Kotka using photogrammetry.
  • Captured photographic data from multiple angles to reconstruct real-world objects as detailed 3D assets.
  • Used Epic Games’ RealityScan to process photographs and generate initial 3D models.
  • Cleaned, repaired and modified photogrammetry-generated models in Blender.
  • Corrected imperfections and unwanted geometry resulting from the scanning and reconstruction process.
  • Prepared 3D assets for use in digital environments and the project’s virtual representation of Kotka.
  • Worked with real-world cultural and public artworks and digitally reconstructed them as 3D assets.
  • Combined photographic capture, photogrammetry and 3D modelling techniques to create usable digital representations.
  • Worked independently on the 3D asset creation process while contributing to the wider goals of the Game Tech Kymenlaakso project.


Tools used
Epic Games RealityScan, Blender, Unreal Engine

Results
Created digital 3D representations of statues and artworks from Kotka as part of the Game Tech Kymenlaakso project. The work contributed to the development of a virtual representation of Kotka and provided practical experience in photogrammetry, 3D asset creation, digital reconstruction and Blender-based model editing. The project allowed me to work with real-world objects and transform them into usable digital assets while developing a hands-on understanding of the workflow from photographic capture to a finished 3D model.

This Task Manager project is a full-stack application I developed to practice backend functionalities and user management features. It allows users to create, read, update, and delete tasks, with role-based privileges (user, moderator, admin) and session-based authentication. The app demonstrates CRUD operations, multiple HTTP methods, and role-specific UI.

What I did

  • Designed and implemented a scalable backend using Node.js and Express to manage tasks and users.
  • Implemented role-based access control: users can manage their own tasks, moderators can view all tasks, and admins can delete any task.
  • Created secure login and registration with hashed passwords and session management for authorization.
  • Developed task functionalities with full CRUD operations (Create, Read, Update, Delete) and different views for completed, pending, and all tasks.
  • Implemented frontend feedback, including color changes and messages when tasks are completed.
  • Designed admin and moderator panels to clearly differentiate privileges and visibility of tasks.
  • Integrated client-side features with backend API to demonstrate full-stack application workflow.

Result
A fully functional task management system showcasing full-stack development skills, including secure user authentication, role-based access control, and responsive UI features. This project demonstrates my ability to independently design and implement backend and frontend systems while applying proper web development practices.
